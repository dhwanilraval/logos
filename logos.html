What happens after Salus finds a vulnerability?

Developers already use coding agents to help fix vulnerabilities, but they still move the work between Salus, the repository, the agent, pull requests, tests, and rescans. After teams approve Logos for all or a selected set of repositories, it connects those handoffs and keeps every existing and new finding moving until Salus confirms closure.
